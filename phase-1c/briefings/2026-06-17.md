## 💼 Business

### **Dow Hits Record High as Oil Slides; SpaceX Surges**  
The Dow Jones Industrial Average reached an intraday record high as oil prices declined, driven by optimism over a potential US‑Iran peace deal. SpaceX shares soared more than 10%, pushing the company past Amazon—and briefly ahead of Microsoft—in market capitalization amid its post-IPO rally.  
*Source: Reuters (June 17, 2026)*

### **SpaceX Acquires Cursor for $60 Billion**  
SpaceX confirmed via an SEC 8-K filing on June 16 that it will acquire Anysphere, the maker of AI coding assistant Cursor, in an all-stock deal valued at $60 billion. The acquisition, closing in Q3 2026 pending regulatory approval, sent SPCX shares up around 17%, making SpaceX the fourth‑largest U.S. company by market cap.  
*Source: BuildFastWithAI / Yahoo Finance (June 17, 2026)*

### **Rackspace Shares Jump 30% on AMD AI Infrastructure Deal**  
Rackspace Technology’s stock surged 30% following a definitive agreement with AMD for a phased rollout of 30 megawatts of AI compute infrastructure. The deployment—incorporating AMD’s Instinct GPUs and EPYC CPUs—will span global data centers from late 2026 through 2028.  
*Source: Yahoo Finance (June 16, 2026)*


## 💻 Technology

### **Mavenir and Red Hat Launch Integrated AI Platform for Network Operators**

*Source: Globe Newswire (June 17, 2026)*

### **Hitachi Expands AI Modernization Efforts with OpenAI**  
Hitachi expanded its collaboration with OpenAI to enhance AI-driven modernization and cybersecurity. Leveraging OpenAI’s Codex agent and Hitachi’s Lumada platform, they aim to analyze and modernize legacy system code while bolstering cybersecurity in mission-critical infrastructure.

### **Vercel and Hugging Face Unveil Innovations in AI Agent Security & Robotics**

## 🌍 World Events

### **G7 Summit Concludes with Key Bilateral Meetings & AI Talks**  
The 52nd G7 Summit in Évian-les-Bains concluded today, June 17. Notable highlights include a bilateral meeting between Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and U.S. President Donald Trump to discuss trade, AI, supply chains, and global security, followed by a working luncheon involving tech leaders and G7 heads to address AI governance.  
*Source: NDTV (June 17, 2026)*

### **G7 Working Lunch Focuses on AI Governance**  
The summit featured a dedicated working lunch between G7 leaders and leading AI executives—including Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, and Demis Hassabis—to discuss safe and effective AI deployment. The first-of-its-kind session aimed to shape voluntary AI governance commitments and prioritize youth safety in AI systems.
