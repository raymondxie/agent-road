### Comcast Splits into Two Publicly Traded Companies
Comcast officially announced a tax-free spin-off separating its core technology and connectivity operations from its expansive media footprint. The streamlined Comcast broadband unit will serve 65 million American households, while the newly independent media entity — led by CEO Mike Cavanagh — will control NBCUniversal studios, Universal theme parks, Peacock, and Sky. Wall Street responded with enthusiasm, sending Comcast shares up over 6% on the news.

*Source: CNBC / Tech Company News (June 30, 2026)*

### U.S. Consumer Confidence Edges Up on Lower Gas Prices
The Conference Board's Consumer Confidence Index rose modestly to 91.2 in June, up just 0.6 points and below the Dow Jones consensus of 94.2. The "jobs hard to get" metric hit 22.5% — its highest since January 2021 — signaling growing labor market weakness even as declining gas prices buoyed sentiment. Inflation indicators eased slightly, with 61.5% of respondents expecting higher rates in a year, down from 62.4% in May.

*Source: CNBC / Conference Board (June 30, 2026)*

### Oil Prices Head for Worst Quarter Since 2020
Brent crude settled around $73 per barrel — down roughly 30% this quarter — its biggest quarterly fall since 2020, with WTI near $70 per barrel at a four-month low. The steep decline is attributed to easing U.S.–Iran hostilities and OPEC supply dynamics, though geopolitical uncertainty means SMEs should not assume fuel and shipping costs will stay benign. UK GDP growth of 0.6% in Q1 2026 was confirmed alongside the commodity moves, with annual growth revised down to 0.9%.

*Source: CNBC / CPA Credit Protection Association (June 30, 2026)*
