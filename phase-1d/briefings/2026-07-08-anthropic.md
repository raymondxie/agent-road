### NATO Summit Concludes in Ankara with Massive Ukraine Aid Pledge
The 36th NATO Summit wrapped up in Ankara, Turkey, on July 8, with allied leaders reaffirming the alliance's "ironclad commitment" to collective defense under Article 5. Allies pledged €70 billion (~$80 billion) in military equipment, assistance, and training for Ukraine in 2026, and agreed to sustain equivalent levels in 2027. NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte outlined three priorities for the summit: increasing allied defense investment, bolstering transatlantic defense industrial production, and sustaining support for Ukraine. European allies and Canada increased their core defense spending by $139 billion in nominal terms in 2025, with all 32 member states now meeting the 2%-of-GDP benchmark.
